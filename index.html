Common questions
Is my journal really private?
Yes. Your entries are stored encrypted in your personal database. We never read them, sell them, or use them to train AI models. The AI only processes entries when you click "Reflect".
How does the AI reflection work?
When you click "Reflect with AI", your last 10 entries are sent to a secure AI model (Gemini) which finds patterns, recurring emotions, and themes, then writes a warm personal reflection back to you.
What's included in the free plan?
5 journal entries per month, all writing features (prompts, mood tracking, word counter), and basic stats. No AI reflections on the free plan.
Can I cancel Pro anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime with no questions asked. We also offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied.
Can I export my journal?
Yes — Pro users can export all entries as a Markdown file anytime from the Entries tab.
